# Udhaar voice

For people who remember the group chat but not who paid for the cab.

- Lead with the moment, then the action: the cab, chai, flat Wi-Fi, concert ticket, trip deposit.
- Write like a helpful friend, not a brand trying to become a meme. One wink is enough; avoid borrowed trend slang and guilt about debt.
- Keep money, dates, privacy, sharing, limits, and deletion literal. Never hide an important consequence behind a joke.
- Prefer short headings and one clear sentence. Buttons should say what happens next.
- Keep the India texture (chai, canteen, UPI-style "I'll send it") without assuming every user has the same currency, city, or language.
- A nudge is a draft, not a message sent by Udhaar. Say that clearly wherever it appears.

Good: “The cab, the split, the ‘I’ll send it later.’”
Too much: “Your bestie’s debt era is giving broke-core.”
